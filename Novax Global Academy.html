About the academy

Language and the world, explained simply

Novax Global Academy is an independent online academy. We teach English together with the subjects behind global news: communication, economics, international relations and international affairs.

Every lesson is short and built around one idea. You get a plain explanation, real examples, useful vocabulary and a practice task. Each lesson also comes as a PDF, so you can study on a phone, print it, or keep it for later.

Lessons are written in clear English, so you learn the subject and practise the language at the same time.

      Questions

      How do I pay?

      Choose a course and press Buy. Payment is handled by a secure checkout provider, so card details never touch this site.

      How do I get the PDFs?

      After payment you receive a download link for the course PDFs by email.

      Is the IELTS course official?

      No. Novax Global Academy is independent and is not affiliated with IELTS, the British Council, IDP or Cambridge. The course teaches skills for the test.

      Do I get a certificate?

      No. The courses are for learning and practice. They do not include an official certificate or test result.

      Can I study on my phone?

      Yes. This website works on phones, tablets and computers, and the PDFs open on any device.

      How long does a lesson take?

      Most lessons take about 10 to 20 minutes, plus time for the practice task.

      What level do I need?

      Each course card shows its level. English Foundations starts from A2. The communication, economics and international relations courses suit beginners.
